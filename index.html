About us

A small Korean company with one simple rule: show the proof.

rekaerB Co., Ltd. was founded in Seoul in 2022. We plan, source and distribute health functional foods for Korean consumers, and we are registered in Korea as a Health Functional Food Distribution & Sales business.

NutriProva is our own brand. It started from a question our customers kept asking: “How much is actually in it, and why?” Too many supplements answer with adjectives. We answer with numbers, sources and plain explanations, including the parts that are not perfect.

Every NutriProva product is made in Korea by a licensed health functional food manufacturer and follows the Korean Health Functional Foods Act, overseen by the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS).

Our principles

How we make a NutriProva product

Four commitments that shape every formula, every package and every page we publish.

01

Show the numbers

Exact amounts per serving and the share of the daily reference value, printed clearly on the package, not buried in fine print.

02

Explain the why

Why this ingredient form, why this amount, why these partners. If we chose it, we can explain it in plain words.

03

Say the downsides too

Taste, color, what it is not. We tell you before you buy, so there are no surprises after.

04

Easy to keep taking

The best supplement is the one you actually take. We design formats that fit a busy day: no water, no pills, one serving.

Quality & compliance

Built on Korea’s health functional food system

In Korea, “Health Functional Food” is a legally defined category. Ingredients, functional statements, labels and manufacturing are all regulated by the MFDS.

I

Licensed manufacturing in Korea

Produced by a specialized health functional food manufacturer in Korea and reported to the authorities as a product item before sale.

II

Regulated distribution

rekaerB is registered as a Health Functional Food Distribution & Sales business, responsible for the product from plant to customer.

III

Approved wording only

We describe functions only with MFDS-recognized statements, and our advertising goes through the industry’s pre-review process.

IV

Checked before release

Each production lot is tested against its specification before shipping, and the shelf life is printed on every box.
